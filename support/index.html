How to play

  • Steer — turn the Digital Crown to slide your car left and right.
  • Start — tap START on the title screen, RETRY after a crash, or HOME to go back and change settings.
  • Dodge — hitting a red car spins you out and costs one of your three lives; lose them all and the run ends. Some weave across lanes.
  • Collect — gold coins add points and count toward garage unlocks. Power-ups are the rounded squares: shield (S), coin magnet (M), and 2× score boost.
  • Zen mode — there are no crashes; tap the screen while driving to end the run whenever you like.

Settings explained

MODE

Cycles CLASSIC → DAILY → ZEN. Daily is the same course for every player, everywhere — a fresh shared course each day that rotates for all players at the same moment worldwide. It never touches your Classic high score.

SENSITIVITY

Cycles the Crown response LOW → MED → HIGH. LOW needs almost four full Crown rotations to cross the road; HIGH crosses it in about one. If steering feels twitchy, go a level lower.

SFX

Toggles the synth sound effects on or off. Haptics stay on either way — near-misses are always a silent tick.

SFX VOL

Cycles the effects volume LOW → MED → HIGH.

MUSIC

Cycles the soundtrack: OFF → RANDOM → each of the six tracks by Binary Aberration. Music ships OFF — pick RANDOM or a track to turn it on; your pick starts playing right away as a preview.

MUSIC VOL

Cycles the music volume LOW → MED → HIGH. Music sits under the effects so the game's cues stay readable.

CAR

Cycles your unlocked car colors. New colors unlock automatically as your lifetime coin total grows — no in-app purchases, ever.

Common questions

Does Wrevvy need my iPhone or an internet connection?

No — Wrevvy is a fully standalone watch app and playable entirely offline. The one optional online feature is Game Center: when you're signed in, a finished Classic or Daily run submits its score to the leaderboards. Signed out or offline, nothing is sent and the game plays exactly the same.

Where are the leaderboards?

Tap LEADERBOARD on the start screen to see the Classic (all-time) and Daily boards. You'll need to be signed in to Game Center on your paired iPhone (Settings → Games, or Settings → Game Center on older iOS versions). Zen runs never post scores.

My sound or music isn't playing.

For the effects, check that SETTINGS → SFX is ON, that SFX VOL isn't set to LOW, and that your watch isn't in Silent Mode. For music, remember it ships OFF — cycle SETTINGS → MUSIC to RANDOM or one of the six tracks, then check MUSIC VOL.

Which watches does it run on?

Any Apple Watch running watchOS 11 or newer — Series 6, SE (2nd generation), Ultra, and later models.

Where is my data stored?

Everything — high scores, daily best, lifetime stats, garage unlocks, and settings — is stored on the watch itself. The one exception is Game Center: while you're signed in, Classic and Daily scores are sent to Apple's Game Center service so they can appear on the leaderboards. Nothing else leaves the watch. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

My scores disappeared. What happened?

Scores are stored with the app, so deleting Wrevvy also deletes them. Reinstalling starts fresh — there's no cloud backup, by design. Anything you posted to Game Center stays on the leaderboards.
