Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026

Wrevvy ("the app," "we," "us") is a game for Apple Watch developed by Satoru Labs. In short: Wrevvy collects no personal data, runs no servers of its own, and keeps what it stores on your device — with one optional exception, Apple's Game Center.

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • We make no network connections of our own, and the game is fully playable offline. The only optional exception is Apple's Game Center (see below), which you control and can leave unused.
  • We do not have accounts, logins, or servers of our own. Game Center, if you use it, is Apple's optional service — not ours.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to anyone — because we never collect any.

What data we collect

None. Wrevvy does not collect, transmit, or receive any personal or usage data, apart from the optional Game Center leaderboard score described below — and that goes to Apple, not to us. There is no server of ours for it to send anything to.

What is stored on your device

To make the game work as you'd expect, Wrevvy saves a small amount of game state locally on your Apple Watch using the operating system's standard on-device preference storage (Apple's UserDefaults):

  • Your scores — your Classic high score and your Daily Challenge best.
  • Your lifetime stats — totals for coins collected, distance driven, runs played, and best combo.
  • Your garage — which car colors you've unlocked and which one is selected.
  • Your settings — game mode, Digital Crown sensitivity, and your sound and music preferences.

All of this stays on your watch at all times. It is not visible to us and is not shared with anyone. The only thing that ever leaves the watch is a leaderboard score, and only if you have chosen to be signed in to Apple's Game Center (see below). If you delete Wrevvy, this information is removed along with the app.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Wrevvy contains no analytics tools, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not build any profile about you.

Network connections

Wrevvy makes no network connections of its own and requires no internet connection to play. It communicates with no server of ours — we don't have one. The single, optional exception is Apple's Game Center: if you are signed in to Game Center, your leaderboard scores are sent to Apple's Game Center service (never to us). See "Game Center" below.

Game Center

Wrevvy includes an optional Game Center feature — two leaderboards (an all-time Classic board and a daily board). It is entirely opt-in, and the game is fully playable without it.

  • Game Center does anything only when you are already signed in to Apple's Game Center, which you manage in your Apple account settings on your paired iPhone. Wrevvy has no separate account or login.
  • When you finish a Classic or Daily run while signed in, Wrevvy submits that run's score to Apple's Game Center service so it can appear on the leaderboard. Zen runs are never submitted.
  • Those scores are handled by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, as part of the Game Center service. Wrevvy operates no servers of its own, receives no personal data about you, and does not store your Game Center identity beyond what the system itself shows on the leaderboard screen.
  • If you are signed out of Game Center, or offline, nothing is submitted and nothing is transmitted — the game behaves exactly as it does with the feature unused.

Everything else Wrevvy stores stays on your device, as described above.

Data sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose any data — because none is ever collected.

Children's privacy

Wrevvy is safe for players of all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws, as there is no data collection to consent to.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Wrevvy handles information, we will update this policy and revise the effective date above. Because the app currently collects nothing and makes no network connections of its own, any change would be reflected here before it takes effect in a new version of the app.
