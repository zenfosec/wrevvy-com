How to play

  • Steer — turn the Digital Crown to slide your car left and right.
  • Start — tap START on the title screen, RETRY after a crash, or HOME to go back and change settings.
  • Dodge — hitting a red car spins you out and costs one of your three lives; lose them all and the run ends. Some weave across lanes.
  • Collect — gold coins add points and count toward garage unlocks. Power-ups are the rounded squares: shield (S), coin magnet (M), and 2× score boost.
  • Zen mode — there are no crashes; tap the screen while driving to end the run whenever you like.

Settings explained

MODE

Cycles CLASSIC → DAILY → ZEN. Daily is the same course for every player, seeded from the date and reset at midnight, with its own "Today's best." It never touches your Classic high score.

SENSITIVITY

Cycles the Crown response LOW → MED → HIGH. LOW needs about two full Crown rotations to cross the road; HIGH crosses it in one. If steering feels twitchy, go a level lower.

SOUND

Toggles the synth sound effects on or off. Haptics stay on either way — near-misses are always a silent tick.

CAR

Cycles your unlocked car colors. New colors unlock automatically as your lifetime coin total grows — nothing to buy, ever.

Common questions

Does Wrevvy need my iPhone or an internet connection?

No. Wrevvy is a fully standalone watch app and works entirely offline — there is no companion iOS app and the game makes no network connections at all.

Which watches does it run on?

Any Apple Watch running watchOS 11 or newer — Series 9, Ultra 2, and later models.

Where is my data stored?

Everything — high scores, daily best, lifetime stats, garage unlocks, and settings — is stored on the watch itself and never leaves it. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

My scores disappeared. What happened?

Scores are stored with the app, so deleting Wrevvy also deletes them. Reinstalling starts fresh — there's no cloud backup, by design.
