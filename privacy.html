Privacy Policy

Effective July 14, 2026

Wrevvy ("the app," "we," "us") is a game for Apple Watch developed by Satoru Labs. In short: Wrevvy collects no personal data, connects to no networks, and keeps the little it stores entirely on your device.

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • We do not make any network connections. The app works entirely offline.
  • We do not have accounts, logins, or servers.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to anyone — because we never collect any.

What data we collect

None. Wrevvy does not collect, transmit, or receive any personal or usage data. There is no server for it to send anything to.

What is stored on your device

To make the game work as you'd expect, Wrevvy saves a small amount of game state locally on your Apple Watch using the operating system's standard on-device preference storage (Apple's UserDefaults):

  • Your scores — your Classic high score and your Daily Challenge best.
  • Your lifetime stats — totals for coins collected, distance driven, runs played, and best combo.
  • Your garage — which car colors you've unlocked and which one is selected.
  • Your settings — game mode, Digital Crown sensitivity, and the sound on/off toggle.

All of this stays on your watch at all times. It is never transmitted off the device, is not visible to us, and is not shared with anyone. If you delete Wrevvy, this information is removed along with the app.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Wrevvy contains no analytics tools, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not build any profile about you.

Network connections

Wrevvy makes no network connections of any kind. It does not require an internet connection and does not communicate with any server, ours or anyone else's.

Data sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose any data — because none is ever collected.

Children's privacy

Wrevvy is safe for players of all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws, as there is no data collection to consent to.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Wrevvy handles information, we will update this policy and revise the effective date above. Because the app currently collects nothing and makes no network connections, any change would be reflected here before it takes effect in a new version of the app.
