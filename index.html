How it plays

The Crown is the wheel.

No tapping, no fumbling, no second hand. Turn the Digital Crown and your car slides across the road — one finger, pure steering. The road is procedurally generated and endless: it narrows and speeds up the longer you survive, so every run ends in a heroic wreck and a "one more go."

CLASSIC

The endless run. Dodge the red cars, scoop the gold coins, survive the squeeze. How far can you get?

High score saved on-wrist

DAILY

One shared course for everyone, everywhere — it rotates for every player at the same moment worldwide. Same road, same traffic — beat your friends fair.

ZEN

No crashing. Traffic ghosts through you, the edges nudge you back on the tarmac. Cruise, collect, tap to end.

The depth

Small screen. Deep run.

Everything on the road earns, saves, or threatens — nothing is decoration.

Near-miss combos

Thread close past a car to start a combo. Chain dodges for escalating bonus points — hold it to ×5 for the celebration.

S M 2×

Power-ups

A shield that soaks one hit, a magnet that vacuums nearby coins, and a 2× boost that doubles your distance score.

Three lives

A slip spins you out and slows you down instead of ending the run — you get three. Lose the last one and it's the end of the road.

Weavers

Some traffic sways across the lanes as it comes at you — and it happens more often the deeper you drive.

The garage

Every coin you've ever grabbed counts. Lifetime totals unlock new car colors:

Feel it, hear it

Haptics tick on every near-miss, thump on every crash. Optional synth blips for coins, power-ups, and new bests — or play silent.

The soundtrack

Six-track synthwave soundtrack by Binary Aberration — shuffle it, pick a favorite, or drive silent. Off by default.

Distance biomes

Drive far enough and the night changes.

Every 1,500 units the road crossfades into a new neon mood. These are the game's actual palettes.

No nonsense

No ads. No sign-ups. No tracking. No servers.

Wrevvy runs entirely on your Apple Watch and plays fully offline. Your scores, your stats, your garage — they live on your wrist. The only thing that ever leaves is a leaderboard score, and only if you're signed in to Apple's Game Center.
